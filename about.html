About BoatRun

Rowing performance tracking — on-water capture, post-session review, squad coaching, live spectating.

1. BoatRun — the app

One native iPhone app covering Row, Cox and Coach. Records stroke rate, pace, catch quality and recovery loss live from motion + GPS, and gives a coach a live squad view. Choose your role each time you open it.

Get BoatRun — TestFlight Beta

A real app, installed from TestFlight (soon the App Store) — no browser install step, works fully offline. On first launch, allow Motion and Precise Location when asked.

2. Viewer — live view of squad position, pace and stroke rate

No install needed — just bookmark it. Get a connection code from the coach and join.

3. Analyser — this site

No install needed — just bookmark it. Use it on a desktop (best) or iPad after a session to review detailed stroke analysis and compare sessions.
