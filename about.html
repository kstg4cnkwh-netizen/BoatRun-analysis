About BoatRun

Rowing performance tracking — on-water capture, post-session review, squad coaching, live spectating.

1. BoatRun — the app

One native app for iPhone and Android covering Row, Cox and Coach. Records stroke rate, pace, catch quality and recovery run live from motion + GPS, and gives a coach a live squad view. Choose your role each time you open it.

Live on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android. Works fully offline. On first launch, allow Motion and Precise Location when asked.

2. Viewer — live view of squad position, pace and stroke rate

No install needed — just bookmark it. Get a connection code from the coach and join.

Open Viewer

3. Analyser — this site

No install needed — just bookmark it. Use it on a desktop (best) or iPad after a session to review detailed stroke analysis and compare sessions.
