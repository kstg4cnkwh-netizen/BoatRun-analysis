BoatRun — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

BoatRun is a set of personal rowing performance tools (the BoatRun app for Row, Cox and Coach, the Analyser and the Viewer). This page explains what data BoatRun collects, how it's used, and how to delete it.

What data is collected

  • GPS location — recorded only while a session is actively running, to calculate speed, pace, distance and heading.
  • Motion sensor data — phone accelerometer and gyroscope, used on the device to detect stroke timing, technique metrics and boat heading. It is saved with the session on your device and in your own Google Drive (if linked); it is not sent anywhere else.
  • Heart rate — if you connect a heart rate strap, or import a Garmin FIT/TCX/GPX file.
  • Session data you create — stroke rate, pace, distance, session names, notes, boat type, race flag and related rowing metrics generated from the above.
  • Account details (optional) — if you sign in with Google or Apple, BoatRun stores the account identifier supplied by that provider, together with your subscription tier, so Pro features can be unlocked on your devices. No password is ever created or held by BoatRun.

Where data is stored

  • On your device — all session data is saved locally in the app first.
  • Your Google Drive (optional) — if you link Drive, sessions are backed up to a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. This folder is private to BoatRun and not visible in your normal Drive view. No one else can access it.
  • Firebase (squads only) — if you join a squad session, your live stroke rate, pace, position and name are broadcast to your coach's screen (and to the Viewer, if the coach shares the squad code) in real time via Firebase, for the duration of the session. This is not stored permanently.
  • Firebase (account record) — if you sign in, a single record holding your account identifier and subscription tier is kept so Pro access follows you across devices.

Location names and weather

To label an on-water session with a general location (for example "Hunter River, Newcastle"), and to show the wind during a session, BoatRun looks up the session's position online:

  • Location name — the session's start position, rounded to about 100 m, is sent through BoatRun's own server to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), which returns the nearest river, lake or town. No name, account or other session data is sent with it. Results are cached for 30 days so the same spot is not looked up again. Location names © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
  • Wind — a position from the session, rounded to about 100 m, and the session's date and time are sent to Open-Meteo to get the wind speed and direction for that hour. No other data is sent.
  • Both lookups happen only for on-water sessions, and only when the phone has a connection. If they fail, the session is saved without them.

Strava integration

If you choose to connect Strava, BoatRun requests permission to upload activities on your behalf. We use this only to create a Strava activity from a session you choose to export — nothing is uploaded automatically or without your action.

  • BoatRun does not read, store, or analyse your existing Strava activities or profile data beyond what's needed to complete the upload.
  • You can revoke access at any time from BoatRun's settings, or directly at strava.com/settings/apps.

Concept2 Logbook integration

If you choose to connect your Concept2 Logbook (erg sessions, Pro), BoatRun asks Concept2 for permission to add workouts to your Logbook on your behalf.

  • When an erg workout finishes, the name screen shows Upload to C2 Logbook. It is on by default once you have connected; tap it to skip that workout. Nothing is uploaded unless you are connected.
  • An upload contains the workout's date, time zone, time, distance, splits or intervals, stroke rate and count, drag factor, calories, heart rate (if recorded), stroke-by-stroke data, your Logbook weight class and the session name.
  • The upload passes through BoatRun's own server on its way to Concept2. Your Concept2 access token is kept in the app on your device.
  • BoatRun uses this access only to add workouts to your Logbook.
  • You can disconnect at any time from BoatRun's settings, which removes the token from your device, or revoke BoatRun's access in your Concept2 Logbook account. Workouts already uploaded stay in your Logbook and must be deleted there.

What is never done with your data

  • No data is sold or shared with advertisers.
  • Data is only passed to the services named on this page (Google Drive, Firebase, Strava and Concept2 if you connect them, OpenStreetMap and Open-Meteo), and only for the purposes described here.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are used.
  • No ads.

Deleting your account

You can delete your BoatRun account and its associated data at any time, directly in the app: open Metrics → Settings → Your Data → Account and tap Delete My Account. You will be asked to confirm twice, and the action cannot be undone.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your BoatRun sign-in (the Google or Apple linked account record held by BoatRun).
  • Your subscription tier / entitlement record stored in Firebase.
  • Your live squad membership entry, so you no longer appear on any coach's screen.
  • All BoatRun session files stored in your Google Drive app-data folder, if Drive is linked at the time of deletion.

Sessions saved locally on your own device are not touched by account deletion — delete those separately from the in-app session log if you want them gone. Purchase and billing records held by Apple or Google are retained by those companies under their own policies and for the period required by law; BoatRun cannot delete them. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that in the App Store or Google Play subscriptions screen.

If you can no longer sign in, email bigwig.91mash@icloud.com from the address linked to your account and deletion will be carried out manually within 30 days.

Deleting individual data

  • Local data — delete sessions from the in-app session log, or clear all app data from BoatRun's settings.
  • Google Drive data — use the "Delete my Drive data" button in Metrics settings (while signed in) to permanently delete all session files from your Drive app folder. Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops BoatRun from reading or writing the folder, but does not delete files already stored there — deletion must be done from within the app while signed in, since BoatRun has no separate server access to your Drive.
  • Strava connection — disconnect from BoatRun settings or from Strava directly; this also deletes the stored access token. Activities already uploaded to Strava remain in your Strava account and must be deleted there.
  • Concept2 Logbook connection — disconnect from BoatRun settings; this deletes the stored access token. Workouts already uploaded remain in your Logbook and must be deleted there.
