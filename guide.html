1. Getting started

New to BoatRun? Start with the Quick Start. It walks you through installing the app, signing in, and recording your first session, for whichever role you are - Row, Cox, Erg or Coach. About five minutes, no settings to change.

This guide is the full reference. The tables below are the parts worth reading on your first day out.

2. Mounting the phone

Orientation

Portrait or landscape both work. What matters is that the phone is flat, screen up, facing the stern (toward the rower), and rigidly attached to the hull. The newer phones (iPhone 15 and newer, and most recent flagship Android handsets) have dual-band GPS which is significantly better at tracking. Due to the reflection of signal from water, carbon fibre and metal components, placing a rubber foam mat under the mount increases performance, particularly on older phones.

  • Portrait - the app uses the Y axis for surge
  • Landscape - the app uses the X axis for surge

Calibration works out which one you're using and pins it. You don't have to tell it anything. If the calibration fixes to the wrong axis, you can force a flip to another axis in system settings instead of keeping AUTO mode.

In landscape, if the phone is turned the wrong way round the app shows ⚠ FLIP PHONE 180° across the top. Turn it round.

Tilt

Flat is best. The app measures your mount angle during calibration and compensates for it, but the compensation gets less reliable the further you go.

  • 0–10° - no meaningful loss of accuracy
  • 10–30° - works, accuracy degrades gradually
  • Over 30° - not supported; the surge signal gets mixed with heave and metrics become unreliable

If the app detects that your current tilt is more than 8° different from your saved calibration, it will offer to recalibrate before you start. Take the offer - it takes 8 seconds.

Cox mode

In Cox role the phone is facing the bow instead of the stern. If you fail to select cox mode, the primary indicator will be unusual stroke curves as shown here.

Position in the boat

Closest to the rigger gives the strongest stroke signal. Anywhere rigid is workable. What ruins it is a mount that flexes or rocks - any movement between phone and hull corrupts every accelerometer metric, and a mount that wobbles near stroke frequency will corrupt stroke ratio and Stroke Char specifically.

Recalibrate whenever you move the phone, change its angle, or switch boats.

3. Roles, Simple and Advanced

One app, four modes. Picked on the launch screen, changeable in HOME → SETTINGS → ROLE.

RoleTab barWhat it does

COXLIVE · CURVE · MAP · INTERVALS · HOMESame as Row, with the accelerometer sign flipped for a bow-facing cox
COACHCOACH · SQUAD · INTERVALS · HOMELive squad map and rower tiles. No recording of your own.
ERGERG · CURVE · SQUAD · INTERVALS · HOMEReads a Concept2 PM5 over Bluetooth. No GPS, no accelerometer - every figure comes from the erg. See Erg mode.

You cannot change role while a recording is running - stop first.

Row, Cox and Erg all broadcast to a squad if connected. Coach mode receives.

The MAP tab shows your own boat, your squad if you're connected, and any sectors, lanes and hazards you have switched on. The SQUAD tab only appears in Erg role, and only once you have actually joined a squad.

Simple and Advanced NEW

New users can pick SIMPLE, a stripped-back version of the app. ADVANCED is the full app described in the rest of this guide.

  • Simple - a plain live screen with Pace, Rate, DPS and HR tiles, plus selectable tiles (time of day and average pace by default). The curve tab keeps fixed tiles. No templates, no LAYOUTS or WORKOUTS tabs, no MAP, INTERVALS or SQUAD tabs. Only the HR chip and Start. On the erg, Simple adds Load Workout and Connect Erg.
  • Advanced - everything.

Switch between them at any time in Settings. Coach role always uses Advanced. Your recorded data is identical in both.

Plan level NEW

HOME → SETTINGS → ACCOUNT & PLAN has a BASIC / PRO / COACH picker. It sets which plan the app behaves as.

  • During the free trial every level is open - pick any one to see exactly how the app looks on that plan before you choose what to buy.
  • After the trial you can step down to a lower level at any time, but never above what you pay for. A coach who wants the simpler rower screens can step down to PRO without cancelling anything.

4. Signing in NEW

Signing in is now built into the app as two short steps, and the app walks you through both. Tap Sign in at the top right of HOME (or the SIGN IN · STEP 1 OF 2 button on the launch screen).

  1. Step 1 - your BoatRun account. Continue with Apple or Continue with Google. This is who you are. It carries your plan between your phone, a new phone and the Analyser, and anchors your free trial so reinstalling doesn't restart it.
  2. Step 2 - link Google Drive. Offered straight after step 1, and the button then reads LINK DRIVE · STEP 2 OF 2 until it's done. This is where your files go - a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive.

When both are done the button shows Drive as linked. Tap it again to open Account & Plan.

Why both

  • Without the account a subscription bought on your phone stays on that phone, and the Analyser won't recognise it.
  • Without Drive sessions live only on the phone. There is no backup, nothing comes back on a new phone, and the Analyser has nothing to open.

Your Google Drive folder

  • Every finished session uploads there automatically
  • The folder is hidden - it does not appear in your normal Drive file list, and no one can read it except you
  • The Analyser is the only other thing that can manage that folder, once you sign in there with the same account and link the same Drive
  • Sign in on a new phone and your sessions come back by themselves - there is no separate restore step any more

Apple and Google

Signing in with Google and with Apple separately creates two different accounts, because your Apple ID and Google account almost certainly use different email addresses. Use the same one on every device. Once signed in, Account & Plan offers Link Apple account or Link Google account - one tap, and it's a spare key to the same account. (Apple sign-in isn't offered on Android.)

Strava and Concept2

Strava is linked separately in SETTINGS → STRAVA SYNC (Pro). It is not needed for anything else.

5. Basic, Pro and Coach

Free trial

Every new install gets 30 days of everything - Pro and Coach, granted by the app. No card, nothing to cancel. The launch screen counts down the days. Sign in and the start date is kept with your account, so reinstalling the app doesn't restart it.

When it expires, you drop to Basic. Nothing is deleted and nothing is locked away - all your recorded sessions, exports and raw data stay fully accessible.

What Basic gives you

  • Pace, Stroke Rate, DPS and Heart Rate tiles
  • All the selectable-tile fields (time of day, elapsed, lap time, distances, average and lap pace)
  • 20 minute recording limit per session - you get a warning with 2 minutes left, then the session finishes and saves itself
  • Full session log and calendar of what you've rowed, full Drive backup
  • Every export - JSON, CSV, TCX and FIT - always free
  • Join a squad with a code
  • Analyser: single-session analysis, always free
  • Viewer: always free, always full

Raw data is never held hostage. If you recorded it, you can export it.

What Pro adds

Pro lifts the recording cap and unlocks every stroke metric, the live stroke acceleration curve on the water and on the erg, ideal stroke template overlays, custom workouts and planning them on your calendar, sectors and courses with live PB racing, lanes, dividers and hazard warnings including club lines, squad erg mode with the live race track, and automatic Strava sync.

On the same account it also unlocks the Analyser properly: multi-session comparison, the video overlay, opening a whole squad at once, and the full stroke template workflow.

What Coach adds

Coach is for anyone running a crew or a squad. Coach includes Pro, so you don't buy both. It adds:

  • Create squad codes - name the squad, share it by QR, optionally with a password
  • Live squad map - every connected rower as a labelled dot, with your sectors, lanes and hazards drawn on it
  • Rower tiles - name, big rate, big pace, plus whichever metrics you choose
  • FREE ROW / RACE switch - individual tiles, or a ranked squad race panel
  • Rower detail on tap - full metrics, live curve, mini map, lap and video for that rower
  • REQUEST CURVE and the STROKE CURVES view - several rowers' live curves side by side
  • LAP ALL BOATS - start an interval on every boat at once
  • Create and push workouts to the squad, on the water or the erg
  • Squad training calendar - plan the squad's week; rowers who follow the squad get it in their own calendar
  • Squad erg races with readiness count and a synchronised start
  • Push a stroke template to the squad mid-session
  • In-app video tagged to the rower and moment
  • Collate and export every rower's session

Rowers do not need Coach. One Coach subscription covers the whole squad - they join free with the code.

6. Recording a session

The LIVE tab

Your main display of selectable metrics. Every tile can be changed right here - tap a tile while the tile lock reads UNLOCKED, or hold it while recording. The LAYOUTS tab is where you set up whole grids.

The CURVE tab (Pro)

The acceleration curve for one full stroke, catch to catch. Last stroke in blue, 5-stroke rolling average in orange. Green fill above zero acceleration, red below.

Three figures sit on the curve itself, each a 5-stroke rolling median:

  • Drive Accel, top left - the highest point of the curve, peak acceleration during the drive
  • Recovery Accel, top right - peak acceleration during the recovery
  • Catch Accel, a third of the way in from the left, bottom of the curve - peak deceleration at the catch. This one reads negative

Four tiles underneath. Tap each one to choose its metric.

Stroke templates on the live curve (Pro)

A template is a target stroke shape built in the Analyser - see Analyser. On the phone it is drawn as a dashed line behind your live curve, continuously re-timed to whatever rate you are actually rowing.

Getting templates onto the phone

HOME → SETTINGS → STROKE TEMPLATES → IMPORT TEMPLATE…. Pick the JSON file the Analyser exported. Re-importing a file you already have replaces it. The list is split into ON-WATER and ERG, each headed with how many are armed. Rename with ✎, delete with ×. Templates live on the phone only.

Arming and showing them

Three on-water slots and three erg slots, kept separately. Arm a template by tapping its row in Settings, or with the TEMPLATES button at the top of the curve. Each armed template gets a coloured pill in the corner of the curve - tap to draw it, tap again to hide it. Only the matching kind is ever shown: on-water templates on the Row and Cox curve, erg force templates on the Erg curve.

Templates from your coach

If you are connected to a squad, your coach can push a template to your phone. It arrives as a pill labelled COACH. Tap it off if you don't want it - your coach sees that you've hidden it.

Templates are boat-class specific. A single scull template means nothing in a quad or an eight.

The MAP tab NEW

A full-screen map of where you are. It turns to match your heading, shows your squad if you're connected, and draws your sectors, lanes, dividers and hazards. The lane readout (see Lanes, dividers and hazards) works on every tab, not only this one.

The INTERVALS tab

The step sequence and per-interval metrics as you complete them. WORK ONLY hides warmup, rest and cooldown rows.

Starting - when the clock actually begins

Pressing START puts the app in a ready state rather than starting the clock, so time spent tied up or backing down doesn't land in your session. Three things release it, whichever happens first:

  • You move. About 20 metres from where you pressed START.
  • You pick up speed. Two GPS readings above walking pace.
  • You take two strokes. The quickest path, and the only one that backdates the clock by a couple of seconds so the first stroke belongs to the session.

Laps

Tap LAP to mark a split. Each lap records time, distance, pace, rate and HR.

Blue - LAPNormal split, or advance to the next workout step. Takes effect the instant you press it.
Light blue - LAP READYPressing will split and arm ready mode. Offered whenever the boat is sitting still, or when the next workout step waits for rowing.
Light green - READYReady mode is armed. The lap clock is held until you start rowing.

Ready mode

Press LAP READY while sitting at the start. The previous lap closes, the new one does not begin. The new lap starts on its own after two strokes at a real rate, backdated about 2 seconds. A single bump won't trigger it. Press the green READY button to start the lap immediately instead.

The lap summary NEW

After each lap a summary card shows the lap's time, distance, pace and rate. A green bar along the bottom counts down until it closes.

  • Tap the card to close it early.
  • Drag the round handle on the bar to change how long it stays up. The full bar is one minute; drop the handle where you want it and that becomes your new timeout for next time.
  • Or set it in SETTINGS → LAP SUMMARY TIMEOUT, 0–60 seconds (default 6). 0 turns the summary off.

The sector result card works the same way - see Sectors and courses.

Stopping and finishing

STOP pauses, RESUME continues, FINISH ends and saves.

The app autosaves regularly. If it closes mid-session (battery, crash, anything) the recording is recovered next time you open the app.

7. Power management NEW

The button at the top right of every live and coach screen cycles through three power modes. Tap it to step to the next.

ButtonWhat it does
⚡ FULLLight screen, everything updating at full rate. Best to read in direct sun.
☾ SAVERDark screen. On an OLED phone black pixels use almost no power, so this is a real battery saving for nothing.
☾ MAX SAVERDark screen, and the display updates less often - tiles every 2 seconds, the curve every 5, the map every 6, the squad race once a second.

Only the display is slowed. Sensors, stroke detection, GPS, recording, lap timing and every alert run exactly as normal in all three modes. Your recorded data is identical.

The choice is remembered. Erg keeps its own setting; Row, Cox and Coach share one.

Overheating

On iPhone, if the phone reports that it is overheating in the sun, a ⚠ PHONE OVERHEATING banner appears and counts down from 15 seconds before switching to the dark screen, which makes far less heat than white at full brightness. Tap DARK NOW to switch straight away or CANCEL to stay light. If it gets very hot, the graphs also slow down. The light screen comes back by itself once the phone cools.

Battery tips

  • A squad link uses the most battery of anything. Leave it disconnected unless your coach needs you live - you can still Send to coach from the log afterwards.
  • Screen brightness is the next biggest drain. Turn it down, or use SAVER.
  • Start long sessions at full charge, and carry a battery pack for long mornings.

8. Heart rate monitor

BoatRun connects directly to a Bluetooth heart rate strap - Polar H10, Garmin HRM, Wahoo, anything speaking standard BLE heart rate. Connect with the ♥ HR chip before you start, or in Settings.

Connect before you press Start. The app reconnects automatically on later sessions once it knows the strap, but the first pairing should happen on dry land.

HR appears as its own tile with a zone bar, is recorded into the session, exports in TCX and FIT, and charts in both the session detail and the Analyser.

Zones are configurable - Friel/Coggan LTHR zones, percentage of max HR, or age-predicted. They sync through your Drive and show in the Analyser too.

10. Workouts

HOME → WORKOUTS (Pro)

On-water and erg are two separate sets

Every workout belongs to one venue or the other, and you only see the set that matches what you're doing. Erg ones carry a red ERG badge. Coach mode sees both and switches with ON WATER / ERG. Drag the grip on a card to reorder your list. Workouts sync through your Drive, so the same list appears in the Analyser.

If a workout is planned for today it shows at the top of the list with a green LOAD button. Workouts copied from a squad plan carry a small yellow dot.

Built in

Race 1000m - open warmup until LAP pressed → 1000m work → open cooldown until you press STOP.

Baseline: Rate Ladder - open warmup → 4 × [1 min 24, 1 min 26, 1 min 28, 1 min 30, 4 min rest] → open cooldown. The last rest is skipped.

Erg: 6 × 500m / 3min - the erg-set default, pushed straight to the PM5.

Building your own

+ NEW WORKOUT → name it → add steps with the coloured buttons underneath.

Notes - an optional box under the name. Whatever you write (or your coach writes) shows when the workout is loaded, and stays available from the 📝 NOTE pill at the top of the live screen.

An on-water workout always opens with a WARMUP and always ends with a COOLDOWN. Erg workouts have neither.

StepWhat you set
WARMUPNothing. Runs until you press LAP.
WORKTIME or DIST, then the value. Optional targets underneath - tap SPM and/or PACE to switch each one on, then set it.
RESTTIME, DIST, or LAP for an open rest that only ends when you press LAP.
COOLDOWNNothing. Runs until you press STOP.

Every value can be nudged with − and + or typed straight in - m:ss for times, plain metres for distances. ▲▼ move a step; × deletes it.

Start - one choice for the whole workout

AFTER 5 SEC (blue)Press LAP at the end of the warmup and a 5-4-3-2-1 countdown starts the first work step.
WHEN ROWING (green)Ready mode. The lap button offers LAP READY during the warmup, then waits on green READY until you are actually rowing.

Repeats

Tick two or more adjacent steps and ↻ REPEATS appears. Give it a count and they become a repeat block. Tick Skip last rest if you don't want the final rest (on-water only).

Loading one from the live screen

The WORKOUT chip opens a quick builder: a straight distance, a straight time, a set of identical intervals with rest, or a list of different intervals - plus your saved workouts. Give it a name to save it, or leave the name blank to run it once. In Coach mode the button reads SEND TO SQUAD.

During the workout

The workout step and time or distance remaining show across the top of the live screen, on the workout tile, and on your coach's screen. END beside the step stops the workout without stopping the session. A 5-second countdown with a tone comes before each work interval (have the phone volume up and the phone off silent), and a lap summary appears at the end of each one.

Target colours and beeps NEW

In a work step with a rate or pace target, the Rate and Pace tiles turn green on target, amber when close, and red when off. After 5 seconds in the red it beeps:

  • Rate - short beeps. Pace - long tones.
  • Two high - rate too high, or pace too fast. One low - rate too low, or pace too slow.

Turn the beeps off in SETTINGS → WORKOUT TARGET BEEPS. The colours stay.

11. Calendar and planning NEW

HOME → LOG → CALENDAR in the app, and the start page of the Analyser.

The calendar

  • Every session you've rowed sits on its day - blue dots for on-water, red for erg.
  • Planned workouts show alongside. Ones from a squad coach carry a yellow dot.
  • Tap a day to see its sessions and its plan. Tap a session to open it.
  • Tap the month name to jump to any month or year.
  • Set which day your week starts in SETTINGS → CALENDAR WEEK STARTS (Monday, Sunday or Saturday). The Analyser follows the same setting.

Planning for yourself (Pro)

Pick a day ahead and add one or more of your workouts to it - in the app's calendar or, easier on a big screen, on the Analyser's calendar. The plan is shared through your Drive, so whatever you plan in one shows in the other.

On the day, when you open the app, a SUGGESTED WORKOUT card pops up with a LOAD button for each workout planned for today. Tap one to load it, or NOT NOW. It only offers on-water workouts when you're rowing and erg workouts when you're on the erg, and it never pops up mid-session.

Following a squad plan (Pro)

Your coach can plan the squad's training on a squad calendar. To get it in your own calendar:

  1. In the Log calendar, tap the SQUAD button.
  2. Pick the squad from your recent codes, or type the code.

The coach's plan, from today onwards, is copied into your calendar when the app opens and every 30 minutes after that. Days the coach changes are brought into line; your own planned workouts on those days are left alone. You don't need to be connected to the squad for this - it doesn't use the live link or its battery.

Planning for a squad (Coach)

In the Analyser, switch the calendar from Mine to Squad, enter the squad code, and plan the squad's days with your workouts. Every rower following that squad gets the update. See Analyser.

Sync to your phone calendar

SETTINGS → SYNC TO PHONE CALENDAR, then pick a calendar on the phone.

  • Planned workouts become all-day events.
  • Completed sessions become timed events with a link that opens the session in the Analyser.
  • Pick your Google calendar and they appear in Google Calendar too. (On iPhone, if none is listed, add your Google account in the iPhone Settings app under Calendar → Accounts.)
  • SYNC COMPLETED EVENTS and SYNC PLANNED EVENTS switch each half on or off. CHANGE CALENDAR moves them.
  • It's one-way: BoatRun owns these events and keeps them up to date. STOP SYNCING CALENDARS removes them.

12. Sectors and courses (Pro)

A sector is a timed stretch of water between a start line and a finish line. Row through it on any session and BoatRun times you automatically, keeps your PB, and races you against it live. HOME → SECTORS.

Getting sectors

  • Draw your own in the Analyser on one of your session tracks - pick a start and a finish on the line you rowed. That track becomes the sector's reference line.
  • Download a course - SECTORS → COURSES lists ready-made courses from boatrun.au, by area, on a map. DOWNLOAD adds it to your sectors. The list is read live from the website, so new courses appear without an app update. Browse them on the Courses page too.
  • Import a sector file someone has shared with you.

Some courses also carry the lanes, dividers and hazards for that stretch of water; downloading the course adds those to your lines as well.

Rowing a sector

  1. Row towards the start, heading the right way. As you approach, a pop-up asks ARM or SKIP.
  2. Once armed, the workout tile becomes the sector tile: metres to the start on top, your PB in the middle, the sector name at the bottom.
  3. Cross the start line - a trill sounds and the clock starts. The middle line now shows metres ahead or behind your PB; the top shows metres to the finish, measured along the reference line.
  4. Cross the finish - another trill, and the result card shows your time and how it compares.

Pre-arm - choose a sector from the list before you go. It then arms by itself every time you approach the start (no pop-up), and re-arms if you turn round behind the start line. Ideal for hands-free head racing and repeat pieces.

Sectors are never shown while a workout is running. If you wander too far off the line, stop for too long or turn back, the attempt is dropped as off-track.

Sector result timeout NEW

There is no Sync with Drive button any more. Once you're signed in with Drive linked:

Everything lives under SETTINGS → YOUR DATA

Freeing up space - STORAGE…

It first reports total use, how many sessions have their full copy on the phone, and how many of those are backed up on Drive versus only on this phone. Then it offers to remove the on-phone copy of sessions that are older than 30 days and confirmed on Drive.

Nothing is deleted. Those sessions stay in your log; open one and it downloads again (you need signal). A session that is not on Drive is never touched.

If the phone fills up anyway

BoatRun will not let full storage cost you a session. When you press FINISH it strips raw debug traces first, then frees the phone copy of already-backed-up sessions, oldest first. If there is still no room the session is held safe in an autosave slot and you're asked to free space and press Finish again - don't close the app. Unsynced sessions are never removed automatically.

Deleting a session

Deleting anywhere deletes everywhere. Swipe a card left in the log, or use the trash icon in the Analyser, and it goes from that device, your Drive and every other device. The confirmation names the session. There is no undo - the hidden app folder is not Drive's normal bin. Deleting looks instant; the Drive copy is removed in the background (or on next signal if you're offline).

Don't delete to make room. Use STORAGE… instead. Export anything you want to keep first - raw data export is free forever.

23. Metrics reference

  • 🟢 Independent - compare across any session, conditions or boat. Accelerometer timing.
  • 🔵 Boat dependent - compare within the same boat.
  • 🔴 Condition dependent - GPS-derived. Tide, current and wind move the number.

🔴 Pace

Boat speed in your chosen unit. Suppressed when GPS accuracy is worse than 25m or the boat is barely moving. Don't compare across days on tidal water unless conditions and direction match.

🔴 Moving Pace

Pace averaged only over the periods the boat was moving. The better number for interval training.

🔴 Distance Per Stroke (DPS)

Boat travel per stroke. Typically 7–11m in a single. At equal pace, higher DPS means a lower rating; a mid-session drop at steady rate usually means fatigue.

🟢 Stroke Rate

Strokes per minute, from recent catch-to-catch intervals. Clears after a rest and returns after two new catches.

🟢 Stroke Ratio

Recovery time ÷ drive time. About 2.0–2.5 at training rates, 1.5–1.8 at race rates. If it collapses as rate climbs, you're shortening the recovery instead of quickening the drive.

🟢 Stroke Char (%)

Where peak drive acceleration falls within the drive: 0% = boat starts accelerating after the catch, 100% = blade exit. Under 40% front-loaded, 40–60% mid-drive, over 60% back-loaded. Smoothed over 5 strokes. On the erg it is where peak force falls within the stroke.

🟢 Recovery Loss

Speed lost to deceleration in the recovery, per second of recovery. Lower is better.

🔵 The catch group

  1. Negative Catch Slope - the gradient down into the check
  2. Catch Accel - the depth of the trough (reads negative)
  3. Positive Catch Slope - the gradient back out
  4. Catch Dur - the width of the trough, from 30% of its depth to back through zero

A steep Negative Slope with a shallow Catch Accel is a sharp, well-placed entry; with a deep Catch Accel it is the boat being stopped. Rising Positive Slope as rate climbs means you're adapting well. Shorter Catch Dur usually means a quicker, more connected catch.

🔵 Catch Consistency

Variation in recent Positive Catch Slope. Lower is more repeatable; it usually degrades before pace drops.

🔵 Drive Time

Peak drive acceleration to blade exit - the back half of the drive.

🔵 Impulse

Total positive acceleration through the drive (Δv). Rising at constant pace means more effort for the same result.

🔵 Drive Accel

Peak forward acceleration in the drive, m/s². Top left of the curve.

🔵 Recovery Accel

Peak forward acceleration in the recovery, after the small dip following the finish. Top right of the curve. Should rise at high ratings.

🔵 Vel Efficiency (%)

How steady the hull's speed is through the stroke. 100% = perfectly steady; 93–97% typical sculling. Higher is better.

🟢 Heart Rate

From a connected strap, with zone colouring.

24. Limitations and warnings

GPS metrics measure over the ground

Pace and DPS measure motion relative to the ground. On a tidal river a 0.3 m/s current changes displayed pace by about 10 seconds per 500m at a 2:00 baseline. The accelerometer metrics are unaffected - they are your reliable technique metrics on tidal water.

Lanes and hazards are an aid, not a lookout

Warnings depend on GPS, which can be several metres out under bridges, near buildings and trees, or on older phones. A hazard is only as accurate as where it was drawn. Keep looking around. Never rely on BoatRun to keep you clear of other boats - it doesn't know where they are.

The mount matters more than anything else

Every accelerometer metric assumes the phone is rigidly attached to the hull. A mount that rocks near stroke frequency corrupts stroke ratio and Stroke Char plausibly enough that you might believe the numbers. Recalibrate whenever you move the phone.

Axis lock after a long rest

If the AX pill stays orange after several strokes, row steadily for 20–30 strokes to let the detector relock.

Crew boats

In doubles, quads and eights the accelerometer sees all the rowers together; stroke detection follows the dominant signal. Catch Slope, Catch Accel and Impulse are not comparable with single-scull values.

No absolute force measurement

These metrics measure the hull's response to blade force, not the force itself. Never compare Catch Slope, Catch Accel, Impulse, Drive Accel or Recovery Loss across boat classes.

Cold, heat and battery

Below about 5°C the phone may throttle. In hot sun it can overheat - see Power management. Start long sessions at full charge.

Squad features need mobile data

Squad, Coach and Viewer need an active mobile connection on the water. Recording is entirely local and continues regardless.

Protecting your data

  • Sign in and link Drive, and check for the Synced badge
  • Export JSON after a significant session
  • Tap Finish rather than force-closing the app

25. Troubleshooting

Stroke detection not triggering Recalibrate with the phone in the mount and the boat still. If the AX pill stays orange for more than a minute, check the mount is rigid and flat.

AX pill shows the wrong axis Settings → SYSTEM SETTINGS → SURGE AXIS → FORCE X or FORCE Y. Only if AUTO is genuinely wrong - check the stroke curve.

Pace high, or dropping out GPS accuracy must be better than 25m. On tidal water pace is over-ground speed.

Recording stopped at 20 minutes Basic limit. Upgrade to Pro.

Tiles say PRO - Upgrade to unlock That metric is Pro-only - or your plan level is stepped down in Account & Plan.

Coach screens keep showing the upgrade Check the plan level in Account & Plan isn't set below COACH.

Sessions not uploading Tap Sign in at the top of HOME and finish both steps. Pending sessions upload automatically when signal returns. Export JSON as a backup if it keeps failing.

Sessions missing on a new phone Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and link the same Drive. Signing in with the other provider creates a different account.

Phone says storage is full SETTINGS → YOUR DATA → STORAGE…. Nothing is deleted.

A session opens slowly, or says it needs signal Its full copy was freed off the phone and is being fetched from Drive.

Deleted a session and want it back You can't. Deleting removes it everywhere.

No suggested workout today It only appears once per day, never mid-session, and only for workouts matching your venue (water or erg). Check the day in the Log calendar.

Squad plan not appearing Make sure you've picked the squad with the SQUAD button in the Log calendar, and that the squad isn't password-protected without you entering the password.

Phone calendar events not appearing Check SYNC TO PHONE CALENDAR is on and points at a calendar the phone syncs. Allow BoatRun calendar access in the phone's settings.

No sector pop-up You must approach the start heading the right way, with no workout running. Pre-arm it to skip the pop-up.

No lane readout Lines only show on Pro, when SHOWN, and within 150 m. Alerts only beep while recording and moving.

Hazard beeps can't be heard Turn the volume up and the phone off silent.

Screen went black but numbers still show That's SAVER or the overheating guard. Tap the power button for ⚡ FULL.

HR strap won't connect Wet the contacts, put it on before connecting, and make sure no watch has already claimed it.

No rowers appearing in Coach Every phone needs mobile data. Confirm the squad code matches exactly.

Rower tiles going grey No recent data. Intermittent grey under bridges is normal.

Battery draining fast Probably connected to a squad. Use SAVER and see Power management.

Android: recording stops when the screen is off Settings → Apps → BoatRun → Battery → Unrestricted. Samsung: also remove BoatRun from Sleeping apps.

Android: HR strap won't pair Allow Nearby devices permission, and turn Location on.

Analyser chart looks empty Click a summary card to bring a metric back, or double-click the chart to reset zoom.

No Personal Bests showing Run Scan All for Records.

Strava upload not appearing Pro only, and Strava must be linked. For a manual upload, export FIT and add it as a Rowing activity.
