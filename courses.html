Courses

Ready-made timed courses. Add one to BoatRun and every time you row through it you get a time, a personal best, and live metres ahead or behind your PB. Courses can also carry the lanes, dividers and hazards for that water — shown on your map, with a warning while you row.

1 · OPEN THE APPIn BoatRun go to HOME ▸ SECTORS ▸ COURSES. Every course on this page is listed there.
2 · DOWNLOADTap DOWNLOAD on a course. It is added to your Sectors on the phone and in the Web Analyser, with its lanes and hazards added to LINES.
3 · ROWRow through it and you are timed automatically. In the Analyser, tap Scan all to find your past times.

Want your course listed?

Draw it in the Web Analyser (Sectors → ✎ Draw), tap Share, and email the file to bigwig.91mash@icloud.com with the river and club name.

To add your club's lanes, dividers and hazards, open the course file in the Lanes & Hazards editor, draw them, download the file and email it the same way.
